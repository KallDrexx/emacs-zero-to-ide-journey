# Preamble

This post reflects on the personal motivations for taking this Emacs journey on and why
this document even exists. It's mostly designed around giving some optional context to
the framing of the articles that will follow.

## Why DE-IDE

I have been programming for over 30-ish years now as a hobby, and then later as a career. While I
don't remember all the tooling I used when I was young, most of that time has been inside
of graphical IDEs. I have been comfortable in many years in JetBrain IDEs for the past
decade, and Visual Studio before that. 

Even during my high school days when I was using Linux, I was using graphical IDEs whose names
I can no longer remember (maybe they no longer exist).
